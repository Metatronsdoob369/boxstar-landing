How it works

Idea to Studio in four steps.

No files to configure. No mechanics to look up. Just describe what you want.

Step 01

Describe your game

Type a prompt. "A zombie survival game where players build bases and fight waves." BoxStar parses your intent and builds a plan.

Step 02

Review the plan

A live game plan fills in as you chat — locked-in features, open questions, the mechanic stack taking shape. Refine before you build.

Step 03

Generate

One click assembles 258+ verified Luau mechanics into a complete project — scripts scoped correctly, dependencies wired, ready to run.

Step 04

Push to Studio

Connect your Roblox account. BoxStar sends scripts directly into Roblox Studio via the Sync plugin, or publishes via Open Cloud. Hit play.
